### PTD revision
- Directive (EU) 2026/1024 of 29 Apr 2026 amends 2015/2302; entered into force 28 May 2026; Member States have 28 months to transpose (=> ~28 Sep 2028) [reported: Garrigues, nexvyra, CMS]. CMS headline: "PTD is no longer really about package holidays... it is about who controls the customer journey."

### CMA AI agents guidance

### UK PTR Amendment Regs 2026 (SI 2026/455)
- LTAs abolished; LTA "type A" (separate services sold via a single trader at one point of contact) folded into package definition; "type B" (targeted linked booking) removed entirely, so those bookings lose LTA protection. Organisers get clearer redress against suppliers. Apply to contracts made on/after 6 Apr 2027 [verified: legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/455 excerpt; reported: ABTA, Weightmans/Mondaq, DWF]. Domestic-package exemption: 65% of consultees supported but no evidence it was enacted -> unconfirmed.
